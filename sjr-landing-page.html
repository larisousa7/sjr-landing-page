The problem

You're not necessarily earning too little. You may just not have a clear strategy for what to do with it.

—

Long hours and shift or roster patterns that leave little time to research property properly.

—

A good income that isn't necessarily converting into long-term wealth.

—

Uncertainty around what you could actually borrow, or what it would take to get there.

—

Conflicting property advice online, and no easy way to tell who actually knows what they're talking about.

—

Not knowing which markets or property types are even worth considering for your situation.

—

A real fear of buying the wrong property and being stuck with it.

—

Wanting to invest but not knowing where to actually start.

—

What SJR actually does

Property investing isn't just about finding a property. It's about having a strategy before you buy.

01

Understand your current financial position.

02

Understand your borrowing capacity and buying power.

03

Define what you're actually trying to achieve.

04

Develop an acquisition strategy around that goal.

05

Identify suitable markets and property types.

06

Access off-market opportunities where they fit the strategy.

07

Conduct due diligence on the numbers, location and risks.

08

Negotiate and assist through the acquisition itself.

09

Repeat strategically, when and if it makes sense to.

Off-market access is one channel used within that process — not the strategy itself. Every property is assessed on whether it fits the plan, not simply because it's off-market.
